# SUPERNOOB-Studios
**https://supernoob20.pythonanywhere.com/** is a website for all things SUPERNOOB Studios.

### Manifesto

SUPERNOOB Studios is a personal project that strives to improve various of my skills by putting them into practice through a myriad of smaller projects. I bring you a website, writing, videogames, art, coding, scanlation, singing, voice acting, and much more. I am always open to comissions, so feel free to get in touch for any inquiries you may have.

On the topic of videogames, I am an indie developer working on two WIP, <url=https://github.com/SUPERNOOB20/>open-source</url> games.

SUPERNOOB Studios' games strive for six general principles:

1. CREATIVITY: I'm not trying to reinvent the wheel. I take inspiration from other people, other stories, other videogames, and from the real world around us. I then try to give it my own personal touch and come up with my own ideas, but it's usually just on top of already existing, settled concepts and genres.
2. CUTE ART: I attempt to deliver cute, colourful art. It's just my personal taste and preference. I find this type of art more fulfilling, both as an artist, and as a consumer. It just makes me happier than other styles :)
3. SIMPLE GAMEPLAY: I try to focus in simple game mechanics, such as those of minigames. Whether it's about clicking, typing, jumping, running, flying, swimming, dodging bullets, etc. the rules should always aim to be as simple as possible. "Avoid the hazards", "collect as many coins as you can", "type the words that appear on screen", "press A to jump, B to shoot, and L to run", etc. 
4. DIFFICULTY: There should always be both easy and unhinged difficulties available for the player to choose from. Easy difficulties make games accesible for those all too familiar with touching grass. I believe in difficulty as the main agent for opt-in replayability.
5. COLLABORATION: SUPERNOOB Studios is a one-woman army. That doesn't mean, however, that I don't receive help - and a lot of it, at that. Learning from others, and then attempting to teaching others in return, is a fundamental part of this studio.
6. EXCELLENCE: I believe in pure effort, research, reading and LEARNING as core values. SUPERNOOB Studios values the 	<ins>learning process</ins> over the 	<ins>final result</ins>. As such, I frown upon the use of generative AI (except perhaps if it's for educational purposes). I do not use AI generated content, unless explicitly stated (for example: it might someday happen that I need to recur to automatic translation to languages I don't know, but this would only happen if lack of manpower prevents me from having human translations instead).

This project is currently in hiatus from lack of motivation (my PC is too potato to handle RenPy and Python smoothly), but I will eventually come back to it (maybe after RRA is released).

[RRA] <b>Reimu's Ribbon Adventure</b> is a <b><i>gameplay-driven</i></b> platformer (more specifically: an IWBTG fangame that is also a Touhou fangame) made for the <url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Boy_Advance>GBA</url> on the <url=https://gvaliente.github.io/butano/index.html>butano</url> game engine. Right now it's just C++, but I plan to make <url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_language>ASM</url> code for it as well (Thumb and ARMv7).

I don't have a release date for them yet, but I would like to have both of them finished by 2027.

I have also set for myself the soft deadline of September 21st, 2025 for a playable RRA demo.

In the future, I would like to develop more C/C++ games to enhance and put into practice my art and coding. The sky's the limit! :3

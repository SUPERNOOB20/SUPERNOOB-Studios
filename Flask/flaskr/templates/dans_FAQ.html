How difficult are the dans?

MY OPINION (as of October 24th, 2025):

雑魚: Good for people who are just now getting into videogames. I tried to put the simplest rhythm and gameplay I could.
Alpha: Good for people who are just now getting into taiko.
Beta: Good for players who already know how to play taiko "properly". In my opinion, if you beat this dan, you can consider yourself a taiko player like any other :)
Gamma: These maps are meant to be the bread and butter for 4 digits - the density is high (and so is the star rating!), but the patterns are aimed to be simple and farm.
Delta: Good for veteran players. These maps require more experience with the game. Prowess with 1/6 or high bpm streams, for example. Any 3 digits should excel at these :) (otherwise, I wouldn't consider you a true 3 digit... lol).


v From here on, all maps are just torture. v

Epsilon: These maps are F*CKED. Mapped with the sole intention of being evilly, rawly difficult, and endlessly jumping at your throat with evil patterns at all times.
神: Absolutely unplayable for me. I can only play these with HT. Good luck. You will need it.



How does it work?

Dans are order from easiest to hardest, so is the easiest, and is the hardest!
You have two ranks: dan rank, and mini-dan rank.
your rank is defined by your hardest mini-dan / dan achieved.

For example: I, personally, am "dan Epsilon 1, mini-dan Delta 1" (as of October 26th, 2025) :3
